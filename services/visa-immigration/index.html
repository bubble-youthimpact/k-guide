Visa & Immigration

This section provides a guide to HiKorea, the Korean Government’s official E-Government for Foreigners.

Apply for E-9 Visa (Non-Professional Employment Visa)

Requirements / Necessary Information:

  1. Valid Passport
  2. Confirmation of Employment (by Korean employer)
  3. Alien Registration Number
  4. Employer’s Business Registration Number
  5. (Optional) Digital Certificate
Instructions: HiKorea.go.kr
  1. Hover over “Information Lookup (정보조회)” on the Top Menu
  2. Select “e-Application Status (전자민원 신청현황)”
  3. Log in using your HiKorea ID and Password
  4. Click on “Application for Permission to Stay (체류허가 신청)”
  5. Choose “E-9: Non-Professional Employment Visa”
  6. Enter your Alien Registration Number, Employer’s Business Registration Number, and Confirmation of Employment

Checking Visa Application Status

Requirements / Necessary Information:

  1. Alien Registration Number (or Passport Number)
  2. (Optional) Digital Certificate
Instructions: HiKorea.go.kr
  1. Hover over “Information Lookup (정보조회)” on the Top Menu
  2. Select “e-Application Status (전자민원 신청현황)”
  3. Log in using your HiKorea ID and Password
  4. A list labeled “e-Application Status” will appear with: Application Type, Submission Date, Current Status, Downloadable Certificate (if applicable).

Apply for a Re-Entry Permit

Requirements / Necessary Information:

  1. Alien Registration Number
  2. Passport Information
  3. Departure and Return Dates
  4. Reason for Travel
  5. (Optional) Digital Certificate or Login Account
Instructions: HiKorea.go.kr
  1. Hover over “Information Lookup (정보조회)” on the Top Menu
  2. Select “e-Application Status (전자민원 신청현황)”
  3. Log in using your HiKorea ID and Password
  4. Click on “Application for Re-Entry Permit (재입국허가 신청)” and fill in Departure and Return Dates, Reason for Travel, Passport Number, and Contact Info.
  5. Save the confirmation number.

Report a Change of Address

Requirements / Necessary Information:

  1. Alien Registration Number
  2. Your Old and New Address
  3. Date of Move
  4. (Optional) Digital Certificate or Login Account
Instructions: HiKorea.go.kr
  1. Hover over “Information Lookup (정보조회)” on the Top Menu
  2. Select “e-Application Status (전자민원 신청현황)”
  3. Log in using your HiKorea ID and Password
  4. Click on “Report Change of Residence Address (주소 변경 신고)” and fill in Your Old Address, Your New Address, Date of Move.
