Identification & Banking

This section provides information about proving your digital identity through Digital IDs and using online banking services.

Requesting a Digital Certificate (공동/금융인증서)

Requirements / Necessary Information:

  1. Alien Registration Number
  2. Bank Account (under your name)
  3. Korean Phone Number (under your name)
Instructions: Signgate.com
  1. Click on “Issue Certificate (공동인증서 발급)”
  2. Select “For Individuals (개인용)”
  3. Click on “New Issue (신규발급)”
  4. Choose “Mobile Phone Verification (휴대폰 본인확인)”
  5. Enter your Name, Alien Registration Number (외국인등록번호), and Korean Mobile Number (must be registered under your name)
  6. Select your telecom provider (SKT, KT, LG U+) and complete the SMS verification
  7. Set a password for your certificate (8–15 characters, including letters, numbers, and symbols)
  8. Choose where to save your certificate (PC hard drive, USB, or mobile phone via QR code transfer)
  9. Click “Issue Certificate” to complete the process

Requesting a Self-Identification Certificate (본인확인서)

Requirements / Necessary Information:

  1. Alien Registration Number
  2. Korean Phone Number (under your name)
  3. (Optional) Digital Certificate
Instructions: Privacy.go.kr
  1. Hover over “Identity Verification Service (본인확인서비스)” on the Top Menu
  2. Click on “Verification History Lookup (본인확인 이력조회)”
  3. Choose a login method: Mobile Phone (휴대폰), Digital Certificate (공동인증서), or Credit Card (신용카드)
  4. Complete identity verification using your selected method
  5. After logging in, review your verification history
  6. Click on “Print Certificate (확인서 출력)” next to the relevant entry
  7. Save or print the Self-Identification Certificate (본인확인서) as a PDF or hard copy

Filing Taxes Online (홈택스)

Requirements / Necessary Information:

  1. Alien Registration Number
  2. Digital Certificate
  3. (Optional) Self-Identification Certificate
Instructions: Hometax.go.kr
  1. Click on “Log In (로그인)” at the top right corner
  2. Select “Digital Certificate Login (공동인증서 로그인)”
  3. Choose your Digital Certificate (공동인증서) and enter your password
  4. Hover over “Year-End Tax Settlement (연말정산)” or “My NTS (My 홈택스)” on the Top Menu
  5. Click on “Simplified Year-End Tax Data (연말정산간소화)”
  6. Perform Self-Identification (본인인증) using your Digital Certificate (공동인증서) or Mobile Phone (휴대폰)
  7. Review your income, insurance, and deduction records
  8. Download or send the data to your employer by clicking “Download File (자료 내려받기)” or “Send to Employer (회사 제출)”
  9. If filing independently, return to the Main Menu and click “File Comprehensive Income Tax (종합소득세 신고)”
  10. Follow the on-screen instructions to enter additional data and complete submission
  11. After submission, save or print your receipt from “Submission Results (신고내역조회)”
